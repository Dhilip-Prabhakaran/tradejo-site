Why TradeJo

Most losses aren't bad analysis. They're broken rules.

You already know your rules. The hard part is following them at 11 am after a loss, with the next candle forming.

"Just one more to win it back"

The revenge trade after a stop-loss, bigger than the one before.

"I'll average it down"

Adding to a loser against your own plan, until it's the day's whole loss.

"Ten trades before lunch"

Overtrading past your limit, where charges quietly eat the profits.

What it does

Your rules, checked before every trade.

Built by a trader, for real trading first: every feature here runs on live trades, every day.

Rules before the click

Daily loss limit, lot caps, trade count, time windows, no averaging. Checked the moment you place an order, not after.

A journal that writes itself

Trades come in from your broker on their own: entries, exits, charges. You add the why; TradeJo does the rest.

Discipline, measured

See P&L from trades that kept your rules against those that broke them. The number that changes habits.

Your playbook, enforced

Write each strategy once: its instruments, size, timing and exits. Every trade is tagged to the setup it followed.

OI data, not calls

Open-interest changes laid out clearly for your own analysis. TradeJo never tells you what to buy or sell.

Capital that adds up

Funds, withdrawals, brokerage and taxes in one place, so your real return is never a guess.
